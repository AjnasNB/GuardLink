# Reach and demonstrated users

The prototype is publicly reachable at https://guardlink.ajnasnb.com. The implementation was exercised with nine labelled test identities controlled for the demonstration. These are distinct wallets, not nine independent participants or a deployed insurance population.

The founder's conversations with Kudumbashree participants and Kerala families shaped the product. We do not claim a verified organization partnership, adoption count, real medical claims or live insurance coverage. A consented pilot would measure whether members can find and understand the next action and whether independent reviewers participate in time.
