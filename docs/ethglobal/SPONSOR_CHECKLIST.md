## Judge evidence and remaining requirements

**Arc Studio Continuity.** The public Studio preview opens the deployed sandbox and reflects the active Arc pool. The source workspace still requires sign-in, and reviewer access needs confirmation. The evidence page links the frontend, backend, architecture, presentation, test log, source and settlement receipts. [Public Studio preview](https://studio.arc.io/share/arc-integration-deployment-sandbox-5d41cde3zs) · [Arc evidence](ARC_STUDIO_EVIDENCE.md)

**Build on Arkiv (existing projects).** The README and linked files cover prior and event work, writer, query, ownership, expiry, privacy, AI use and MIT licensing. The Node Tooling criteria have separate companion notes. [Arkiv judging files](../../arkiv/README.md)

**Arkiv + Swarm (existing projects).** The linked evidence covers the public entity and ciphertext address, Bee gateway, upload and read, access checks and demo timestamp. The gateway operator still needs to confirm the actual postage batch and buyer or provider. [Swarm verification](../../arkiv/swarm.md)

**Build with Uniswap (Continuity).** Public MIT code, `FEEDBACK.md`, the form confirmation and line-level integration pointers are linked to the successful swap, bridge and Arc deposit. [Uniswap evidence](CONVERSION_RUN.md)

## Video and deck

Target a three-minute video, within the required 2–4 minute upload range, at 1080p. Use the builder's own narration. The published instructions reject AI voiceover and sped-up footage. Show the working case, not only slides. The deck should support the story and architecture rather than replace the demo.

## Baseline and integrity

Preserve Git history. Disclose the inherited project in the Continuity submission even if the product pitch does not discuss its historical brand or chains. Record actual AI assistance. Publish only authorized source and synthetic evidence. No private keys, contact lists, real medical documents or local memory belong in the submission repository.
