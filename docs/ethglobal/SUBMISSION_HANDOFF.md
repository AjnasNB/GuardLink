## Arc House showcase copy

**GuardLink — Cooperative health coverage on Arc Testnet**

After my father's eye surgery, our family borrowed while waiting for reimbursement. We had the paperwork, but we still couldn't see what was holding up the claim. By the time the money arrived, it felt like a favour. That experience is why I started GuardLink.

Over the past year, I reached 10,000 people directly and collected around 200,000 prospective contacts through outreach. Conversations with women and families in Kerala's self-help communities shaped our early cooperative coverage prototype. I'm continuing that work at ETHGlobal Mumbai in the Continuity Track and deploying the new version on Arc Testnet.

Members contribute test USDC, receive a policy NFT and track a private claim. Human jurors decide, an independent panel can hear an appeal, and the community can verify confirmed payouts on Arc. A recent live test deposited 1 test USDC and minted Policy #7. The public Studio preview reflected the new policy count.
