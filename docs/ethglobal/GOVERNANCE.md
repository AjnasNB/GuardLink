# GuardLink community governance

[Open community governance](https://guardlink.ajnasnb.com/app/governance/) with a GuardLink member wallet.

Browse [public proposals](https://guardlink.ajnasnb.com/governance/proposals/) without signing in. The [screenshot walkthrough](DAO_WALLET_WALKTHROUGH.md) covers setup, role switching, jury assignments, votes and receipts.

| Page | What members can do |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | See proposal activity and how votes pass. |
| Proposals | Filter by voting status and open a proposal to see the change, the vote count, and the deadline. |
| New proposal | Explain the change, impact, timing, and conflicts. Enter the proposed policy terms and review the draft before signing. |
| Treasury | Read free treasury, approved payouts, and open claim exposure from the Arc pool. Follow the fund for public milestones. |
| Voting rules | See the eligibility snapshot, quorum, support threshold, and execution rules. |

The proposal format draws on [ArbitrumDAO's proposal guide](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/how-tos/create-submit-dao-proposal). Its public [forum](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/) and [governance portal](https://alt.gov.arbitrum.foundation/) illustrate how to separate discussion, proposals, and treasury information. GuardLink's proposal text is public. Members should keep personal and medical information out of it.

GuardLink uses one direct vote per member eligible at the proposal snapshot. ArbitrumDAO delegates token voting power. GuardLink can consider revocable delegation for cooperative proposals if members struggle to participate directly. Delegation would require contract changes and tests for eligibility, revocation, and double voting. Juror votes remain a separate decision on individual cases.
